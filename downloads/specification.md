# Frozen analysis specification

**Frozen 20 September 2026, before any release model was estimated.**

This is an *exploratory analysis specification*, not a preregistration. It was written
after the data were inspected for coverage and item validity, and after the corrected
inference helper was tested, but before any coefficient in this release was computed.
Where a rule was chosen with knowledge of the coverage tables, that is said so here.

---

## 1. Estimand

The default estimand is **the equal-weight average of estimates calculated separately
within the participating OECD systems**. Every published figure names its own
contributing country set. A restricted set is never called "the OECD average".

This matches OECD's own construction — the Reader's Guide to *PISA 2025 Results
(Volume I)* states that the OECD average is the arithmetic mean of the respective
country estimates, and warns that it refers only to the members included in that
particular comparison.

Uncertainty is the Fay-BRR sampling variance (80 replicates, k = 0.5, multiplier 0.05)
plus Rubin imputation variance over the 10 plausible values, formed **within each
country first** and then combined across independent country designs as
`sum_c a_c^2 * cov_c`. Intervals are `estimate ± 1.96 SE` and are labelled approximate.
They describe sampling and imputation uncertainty for the systems included — not a
random sample from a universe of countries.

## 2. What the file does and does not support

Read from `CY09_MS_STU_PUF.sav` (sha256 `6f6fa1e1…fc379`), 755,721 records, 90 systems:

- 10 plausible values each for SCIE, READ, MATH; 80 replicate weights. Verified, not assumed.
- `CNT`, `CNTSCHID`, `CNTSTUID`, `STRATUM`, `SUBNATIO` are fixed-width strings and stay strings.
- **The file declares no user-missing ranges, and the special codes 95–99 (and 5–9 on
  `ST324Q11JA`) never occur in the data.** Every non-substantive response — valid skip,
  system missing, not administered, invalid, no response — is collapsed to system
  missing. The value labels document the five categories; the data does not carry them.

**Consequence, carried into the limitations section:** in this file it is *not possible*
to separate a module that was not administered from an item a student was asked and did
not answer. Coverage is therefore reported as observed valid responses against all
released records for that country, with the denominator named. No country is described
as "not administered", and no absent module is ever converted to a zero.

## 5. Support and exclusion rules, fixed before estimation

1. **Category support (primary screen model).** A system contributes only if every one
   of the six `IC177Q02JA` categories has at least 30 records in that system's analytic
   sample. This is *our* analytic stability rule, not an OECD rule, and every excluded
   system is reported.
2. **Rank.** A rank-deficient weighted design raises an error carrying country, model
   and replicate. The affected estimate is published as `status = unsupported` with a
   reason and no number. It is never returned as zero or NaN.
3. **Replicate support.** Every group mean requires positive weight in every replicate.
4. **Sparse cells in the descriptive AI distribution** are flagged with their counts and
   kept visible, not deleted.
5. **General index** = unstandardised mean of the 11 battery items excluding Q02,
   requiring at least 8 valid items on the 1–4 scale. Q02 is excluded because it is
   separately controlled and must not sit on both sides of the comparison.
6. **ESCS quartiles** are weighted, computed within country, recomputed inside every
   replicate, and disjoint: Q1 at or below the weighted 25th percentile, Q4 above the
   weighted 75th. Ties are not broken, so realised group sizes are reported rather than
   assumed to be quarters.

## 6. Sensitivities, fixed before estimation

- **S1 Cautioned systems.** Re-estimate the principal contrasts excluding OECD systems
  carrying an applicable official PISA 2025 caution: CAN, NLD, NZL, NOR, USA. Only
  those actually contributing are operative.
- **S2 Low module coverage.** Re-estimate excluding systems whose weighted valid share
  on the exposure item is **below 60%** of that system's released records. This
  threshold was chosen after reading the coverage table and **before** estimating any
  coefficient. On `screens_main` it removes GBR (6.1%), ISR (42.7%) and USA (51.1%).
- **S3 Leave-one-country-out** for every principal contrast.
- **S4 ESCS-squared** on the identical `screens_main` records.
- **S5 Complete 11-item battery** subset for the nested AI models, plus the main
  specification re-fitted on that same subset, so sample restriction is separated from
  adjustment.
- **S6 Within-school** comparison on `screens_school`, conditional on the diagnostics in
  §7 passing.

## 7. Conditional and excluded work

- **Within-school model** ships only if school identifiers validate, the within-school
  design has full rank in every replicate, and the demeaning matches an explicit-indicator
  fit. Otherwise it is omitted and school composition is stated as unresolved.
- **Reading and mathematics** are a secondary robustness appendix, restricted to the same
  supported country set as science. Domain coefficients are not compared as though their
  difference had been tested.
- **Disengagement** is optional appendix material and is dropped if it would delay a gate.
- **Excluded from the public release entirely:** mixed-task process/attention/patience
  claims, 2050 forecasts, migration and management arguments, school-policy effects, and
  the unrepaired decile heat maps.

## 8. Language rules

Confidence is self-report, not demonstrated AI literacy. ESCS is economic, social and
cultural status, not income or household wealth; groups are the lowest and highest ESCS
quartile, not the poorest and richest. Less-than-weekly chatbot use is not non-use.
No causal claim, no safe-hour threshold, no inference of no effect from nonsignificance,
and no conversion of score gaps into school years.
